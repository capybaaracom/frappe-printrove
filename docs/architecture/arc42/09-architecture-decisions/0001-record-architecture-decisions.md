# ADR 0001: Record Architecture Decisions

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-08-10

## Context
Architectural decisions for `frappe_printrove` must be recorded systematically to maintain architectural clarity, prevent knowledge loss, and ensure consistency across the development and operations teams.

## Decision
We will use Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) stored under `docs/architecture/arc42/09-architecture-decisions/` formatted according to standard arc42 conventions with zero-padded sequential numbering.

## Consequences
### Positive
- Documents technical context and rationale behind critical design choices.
- Ensures all architectural changes undergo explicit review and evaluation.

### Negative & Risks
- Requires continuous maintenance when architectural patterns evolve.
- Mitigated by incorporating architecture synchronization checks into development workflows.
